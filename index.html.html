The mission

Quality education is power.
Access is everything.
We're the students making sure it reaches other students who deserve it too.

What we do

Fundraise for named students, spotlight their stories, and mobilize NYC high schoolers to help.

Who we back

Under-served students in Nepal and India — through the Just for Humanity Tuition Scholarship Program.

Where donations go

Directly through the Just for Humanity 501(c)(3). 95% program services, 5% operations.

The gap we're closing

Education is a human right. Right now, it isn't reaching everyone.

How we help

Three things we actually do — beyond posting about it.

01 /

Fundraise.

Thrift sales in Washington Square Park. Bake sales. Book sales. Corporate matching. Every dollar routes through J4H's tax-exempt program with 95% going to student services.

02 /

Spotlight stories.

Our student interview series follows named students in three phases: origin, progress, future. Real reporting from our team — deep storytelling, not stock photos.

03 /

Mobilize students.

NYC high schoolers on our team run posts, reels, stories, interviews, and events. Real volunteer hours. Real skills. Real portfolio work for college.

Volunteer

Join the team. Earn real hours. Build real skills.

We're always looking for NYC high school students who want to work on social, content, interviews, or events. Comfort-first — face-on-camera is always optional. Deadlines are soft. The work is real.

  • Portfolio-worthy work in social, design, journalism, and ops
  • Verified community service hours (documented)
  • Real access to running a nonprofit chapter
  • Group chat that actually feels like a team
